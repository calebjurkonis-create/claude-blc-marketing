# Seminar follow-up

The money is in the appointments, not the RSVPs. This skill covers every touch from the RSVP call to the booked consultation. Read `CLAUDE.md` first for voice and the disclaimer.

## 1. Collect the details
Venue, the two dates/times, meal, and whether any attendees gave emails (most RSVP by phone, so texts from Google Voice are the default channel).

## 2. Write these pieces
1. **RSVP call script** (answering 407-735-6371): warm greeting, which night, how many guests (you + 1), any dietary needs, best number for a reminder text, "Are you coming up on 65 or already on Medicare?" (fine to ask on a call; it's not an ad). Under 60 seconds.
2. **Confirmation text**, sent right after the call: night, time, venue + address, "reply C to confirm."
3. **Reminder texts**: 3 days before, and morning-of ("We're saving your seats tonight at 6!").
4. **Day-after, attended**: thank you, one useful takeaway, and the ask: book a 30-minute personal review. Offer two specific time slots.
5. **Day-after, no-show**: no guilt, invite to the next night/next workshop, or offer a one-on-one instead.
6. **Day 7 nudge** for attendees who haven't booked.
7. **Optional email versions** of 4–6 if emails exist. Create as Gmail **drafts** only, never send.

Rules: texts ≤ 300 characters, first name only, one ask per message, sign as "Caleb & Moose". Include the disclaimer on anything that mentions Medicare plan choices.

## 3. Save + summarize
Save to `seminars/<venue-slug>/followup.md` with a simple timeline at the top (T-3, T-0, T+1, T+7). Draft only; Caleb sends.
