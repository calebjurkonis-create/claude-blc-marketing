**Timeline**
| When | Touch | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| On RSVP call | Script below + "how did you hear about us?" | Phone |
| Right after call | Confirmation | Text |

## 1. RSVP call script (Google Voice 407-735-6371)

> "Hi, thanks for calling about the dinner workshop! This is Caleb [or Moose]. Who do I have the pleasure of speaking with?
>
> Great, [Name]. We've got two nights at The Grove House: [DATE 1] or [DATE 2], both at 6. Which works better?
>
> Will anyone be joining you? *(dinner covers you + one guest)*
>
> Any dietary needs we should know about?
>
> Quick one so we tailor the night: are you coming up on 65, or already on Medicare?
>
> And how did you hear about us: the postcard, Facebook, or a friend?
>
> Is this the best number to text you a reminder? Perfect. You're all set for [night] at 6. We'll save your seats. See you there!"

Log each call: name, night, # guests, T65 vs. on Medicare, **source (postcard / Facebook / friend)**, phone.
