# Grove House lunch: Google Form for online RSVPs

**Live form:** https://forms.gle/CHd8XdhEEyJr5EU38 (linked from the website's "RSVP Online" button)

Build at **forms.google.com** → **Blank form** (about 5 minutes). Answers go to a Google Sheet automatically.

**Form title:** RSVP: Free Medicare Lunch Workshop
**Description:**
> Lunch is on us, for you and a guest. Sat, Nov 14 or Mon, Nov 16 · 12:00 PM · The Grove House (formerly Sawgrass Tavern), 810 Marilee Pl, The Villages. Questions? Call 407-735-6371.

## Questions (type → settings)

1. **First and last name**: Short answer · Required
2. **Best phone number**: Short answer · Required
3. **Email (optional)**: Short answer
4. **Which day works for you?**: Multiple choice · Required
   - Saturday, November 14 at 12:00 PM
   - Monday, November 16 at 12:00 PM
5. **How many people (including you)?**: Multiple choice · Required
   - Just me
   - 2 (me + a guest)
6. **How did you hear about us?**: Multiple choice · Required
   - Postcard in the mail
   - Facebook
   - A friend or family member
   - Other
7. **Consent**: Checkboxes · Required · one option:
   - I agree Caleb & Moose may contact me by phone, text or email about this workshop. No obligation to enroll.

## Settings

- **Confirmation message** (Settings → Presentation):
  > You're on the list! We'll call or text to confirm your seats. Questions? Call 407-735-6371. – Caleb & Moose
- **Responses tab → Link to Sheets → Create a new spreadsheet** named "Grove House RSVPs Nov 2026".
- **Responses tab → ⋮ → Get email notifications for new responses** so you see each RSVP right away.
- **Send → link icon → Shorten URL → Copy.** Send that link to Claude to put on the website button.

Online RSVPs get a call or text from Caleb or Moose within a day (use the confirmation text in `followup.md`).
