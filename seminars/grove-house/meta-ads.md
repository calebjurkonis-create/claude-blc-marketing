## Campaign setup checklist (Ads Manager, in this order)

1. **Create campaign → Leads.** Turn on **Special Ad Category → Financial Products & Services** before anything else. It can't be added later.
2. **Campaign name:** `grovehouse_workshop_[month]`
3. **Ad set A: Calls.** Conversion location **Calls**, number 407-735-6371.
   - Location: **15 miles around 810 Marilee Pl** (covers The Villages, Lady Lake, Fruitland Park, Wildwood, Oxford, Summerfield and most of Leesburg). That 55+ footprint is your age filter, since Meta locks age to 18–65+ for this category.
   - Audience: broad / Advantage+. Placements: Advantage+.
   - Budget: **$30/day** Tue 11/3 – Tue 11/10, then **$40/day** Wed 11/11 – Fri 11/13 for the final push. **Last day of ads: Fri 11/13** (end 11:59 PM).
4. **Website test (replaces the instant-form idea).** A second, small campaign that sends people to the RSVP website, which has both the call button and the online form.
   - **Create campaign → Traffic**, again with **Special Ad Category → Financial Products & Services**. (A Leads campaign can't send people to the website without a Meta pixel, and the Canva site can't hold one.)
   - Campaign name: `grovehouse_workshop_website`. Optimize for **Landing page views**.
   - Same 15-mile location, broad audience and Advantage+ placements as ad set A.
   - Website URL: `https://otter-k66vr8.my.canva.site/`. Display link: `otter-k66vr8.my.canva.site`. CTA button: **Sign Up**.
   - Use the website versions of the two ads (below).
5. **Budget split (same ~$360 total):**

   | Dates | Calls (A) | Website | Per day |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Tue 11/3 – Tue 11/10 | $20/day | $10/day | $30 |
   | Wed 11/11 – Fri 11/13 | $27/day | $13/day | $40 |

   Day-4 check (Fri 11/6): if one route has 2+ RSVPs and the other has none, move the money to the winner.
6. Add the two image ads below (and the video once it exists) to each ad set. Name them `grovehouse_<angle>_<format>` (website versions: `grovehouse_<angle>_image_web`).
7. **Planned spend:** ~$360 over 11 days (about half a postcard drop). **Launch: Tue, Nov 3** (page must be live and linked by Mon, Nov 2).

**How to tell which route worked:** phone RSVPs come from the call ads, postcards or the site's call button, so ask every caller "How did you hear about us?" (it's in the RSVP script). Google Form RSVPs come from the website; the form asks the same question.

## Creative briefs

**Images (4:5 / 1080×1350):** both built in Canva with Caleb & Moose's headshots, venue, "Sat, Nov 14 or Mon, Nov 16 · 12:00 PM" and "CALL TO RSVP 407-735-6371". For the next seminar, copy and edit the date line.

Dropped on 2026-10-26: the "sign-up timeline" ad (made it look fine to sign up late) and the "Advantage vs. Supplement" comparison (too much to take in, not enough of a hook). The designs are still in the Canva folder.

**Video: Caleb & Moose on camera (20–30s). This is the ad most likely to win.**

Script:
> **Caleb:** "Hi, I'm Caleb, and this is Moose."
> **Moose:** "Medicare doesn't have to be confusing."
> **Caleb:** "Join us for a free lunch workshop at The Grove House. We'll cover when to sign up, Advantage versus Supplement, and Social Security timing."
> **Moose:** "Lunch is on us, so bring your spouse or a friend."
> **Both:** "Call 407-735-6371 to save your seats. See you there!"

On-screen text to add in editing: "FREE LUNCH WORKSHOP" (first 3s), venue + "Sat 11/14 or Mon 11/16 · Noon" (middle), "CALL 407-735-6371" (last 5s), disclaimer (small, last 3s).

Filming checklist:
- [ ] Phone held **vertical**, at eye level (stack books or use a $15 tripod)
- [ ] Face a window or shoot outside in shade; no bright window behind you
- [ ] Quiet room; phone within 3–4 feet so the mic picks you up
- [ ] Stand side by side, shoulders angled in, smile before you start talking
- [ ] Do 3–4 takes; it's fine to read lines off a laptop behind the phone
- [ ] Keep it under 30 seconds; first 3 seconds should already show both faces
- [ ] Bonus: 5 seconds of The Grove House dining room/food to cut in (b-roll)
- [ ] Upload the best take to the Canva folder; I'll add captions and text there

**Placeholder videos (until you film):** animated versions of the 3 images. Canva's **Animate** button does this in about a minute each (open the design → Animate → pick "Rise" or "Fade" → Share → Download → MP4). Weaker than the two of you on camera, but free to test.

## Break-even math

October 2026 Grove House postcards (actuals, also in the [Seminar Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bgtSi9PC-Ra-JDUYlSBvNZhKAB44R0B3PuuGyb7l_cg/edit)):
- $693.70 for 1,008 cards → 9 RSVPs → 8 attended → 7 appointments.
- **$77 per RSVP, $99 per appointment** (before the meal). That's the bar Meta has to beat.
- The room converts: 87.5% of attendees booked. The constraint is getting more people in the door, which is what Meta is for.
- On the ~$360 budget, Meta beats postcards at **5+ RSVPs**. Check on day 4: on pace for that, keep going; on pace for under 3, swap the weakest angle.
- Best test: run Meta **alongside** the next postcard drop and ask every caller "How did you hear about us?" (it's in the RSVP script).
