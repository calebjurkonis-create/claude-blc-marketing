## Campaign setup checklist (Ads Manager, in this order)

1. **Create campaign → Leads.** Turn on **Special Ad Category → Financial Products & Services** before anything else. It can't be added later.
2. **Campaign name:** `grovehouse_workshop_[month]`
3. **Ad set A: Calls.** Conversion location **Calls**, number 407-735-6371.
   - Location: **15 miles around 810 Marilee Pl** (covers The Villages, Lady Lake, Fruitland Park, Wildwood, Oxford, Summerfield and most of Leesburg). That 55+ footprint is your age filter, since Meta locks age to 18–65+ for this category.
   - Audience: broad / Advantage+. Placements: Advantage+.
   - Budget: **$30/day**, start 12 days before `[DATE 1]`, end 3 PM on `[DATE 2]`. Bump to **$40/day** for the last 4 days.
4. **Ad set B (optional test): Instant form.** Same location and budget split ($15/$15 with A).
   - Form fields: First name, Phone. Custom question: *"Which night works best?"* → `[DATE 1]` / `[DATE 2]`. (Age questions aren't allowed in this category.)
   - Thank-you screen: "You're on the list! We'll text to confirm. Questions? Call 407-735-6371."
5. Add the three ads below to each ad set. Name them `grovehouse_<angle>_<format>`.
6. **Planned spend:** ~$390 over 12 days (≈ half a postcard drop).

## Creative briefs

**Image (all three angles, 4:5 / 1080×1350)**
- Top band: "FREE DINNER WORKSHOP" (same look as the Grove House mailer).
- Middle: angle hook in big type. (1) "When should you sign up for Medicare?" (2) "Advantage or Supplement?" (3) "Dinner's on us."
- Bottom: venue, `[DATE 1]` / `[DATE 2]` · 6 PM, "CALL TO RSVP 407-735-6371".
- Best photo: Caleb & Moose together, smiling, at The Grove House or in front of a room. Real photos beat stock with this crowd.
- Keep text readable at arm's length: 3 lines max, high contrast.

**Video (15–20s, vertical 4:5, captions burned in)**
- 0–3s: Caleb or Moose to camera at The Grove House: "Medicare doesn't have to be confusing."
- 3–12s: "Join us for a free dinner workshop. We'll cover when to sign up, Advantage vs. Supplement, and Social Security timing."
- 12–18s: Dining room b-roll + on-screen dates. "Bring a guest. Call 407-735-6371 to save your seats."
- Shoot on a phone, landscape-free, good light. Authentic beats polished.

## Break-even math

- Last postcard drop: ~$700 for ~1,000 pieces.
- **Fill in:** how many RSVP calls did the October Grove House mailer get? → `[N]`
- Postcard cost per RSVP = $700 ÷ `[N]`. Example: 35 RSVPs = **$20 per RSVP**. That's the number Meta has to beat.
- Watch after day 4: if cost per call is under that bar, keep going; if it's 2× over, swap the weakest angle.
- Best test: run Meta **alongside** the next postcard drop and ask every caller "How did you hear about us?" (it's in the RSVP script).
